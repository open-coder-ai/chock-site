# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

The chock site builds pages and policy files that adopters copy into their repos;
a vulnerability here can reach every visitor and every repo that uses its output.

- **Preferred:** open a private security advisory:
  <https://github.com/open-coder-ai/chock-site/security/advisories/new>
- Do **not** open a public issue for exploitable findings.
- Include: the affected page or file, reproduction steps, and impact
  (e.g. HTML or Markdown injection, a CSP bypass, a third-party request).

Expect an acknowledgment within 7 days and an initial assessment within 14 days.
A vulnerability in the chock CLI or a catalog policy belongs in
[chock](https://github.com/open-coder-ai/chock/security/advisories/new) or
[chock-catalog](https://github.com/open-coder-ai/chock-catalog/security/advisories/new).
